Option A — what changed

  • Google trust bar added. 4.9, gold stars, and the review count now lead the section instead of hiding in a button at the bottom.
  • Per-card Google mark + "Verified." Replaces the muted "Google Review" label — reads as third-party proof at a glance.
  • Relative dates added. "3 weeks ago" signals the business is active right now. Currently there is no recency signal anywhere.
  • Write-a-Review CTA merged in. Kills the separate section that sits directly below today — two review sections back to back dilute each other.
  • Grid untouched. Same hairline 3-up layout, so nothing about the page rhythm shifts.

Option B — everything in A, plus

  • Pull-quote ticker. Ten of the shortest real reviews, looping slowly. Every quote is verbatim — nothing truncated.
  • Why short ones only. The three featured reviews stay full-length and readable; the ticker carries the volume signal that a 3-card grid can't.
  • Pauses on hover and stops entirely for visitors with reduced-motion enabled.
  • Pure CSS keyframes. No React or Framer Motion needed — the 410 site stays a zero-build static page.
  • Trade-off: more movement on a page whose brand reads steady and dependable. Worth a look on mobile before committing.
