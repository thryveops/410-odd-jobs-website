Common Questions

Got Questions? We Have Answers.

Everything you need to know about our Howard County junk removal and hauling services.

How do you price your services?

Our pricing is primarily based on the volume of space your items take up in our truck. Depending on the job, flat or hourly rates may apply. No hidden fees, ever.

Are you insured?

Yes, 410 Odd Jobs LLC is fully insured. We take the safety of your property and our team very seriously, giving you peace of mind from start to finish.

Do you take hazardous materials?

While we no longer remove hazardous materials ourselves, Howard County provides residential drop-offs every Saturday morning from April through late summer. Don't let that stop you from reaching out, though! Many items aren't actually hazardous, and we can still clear out the rest of your junk. Contact us to discuss your project, and we'll take care of everything else.

Do you offer same-day service?

We often do! Depending on our schedule in Howard County, we can frequently accommodate same-day or next-day requests. Call (443) 518-6074 for immediate availability.

Where does the junk go?

We prioritize responsible disposal. Items in good condition are often donated to local charities, and we recycle as much as possible before heading to the landfill.
